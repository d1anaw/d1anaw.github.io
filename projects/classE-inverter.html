HF/VHF Class-E Inverter at 30 MHz

Power Electronics / High-Frequency Power Amplifiers

Skills Developed

LTSpice, MATLAB, High-frequency PCB layout (Altium Designer), PCB validation, High-frequency PCB design

Project Description

This project is part of my research conducted via the Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP) at MIT. Unforunately, I cannot share many details on the project at this time for confidentiality purposes. However, I can share a basic version of Class-E inverter that I developed and verified in simulation. These results are presented in the MARC 2026 conference, and you can find the poster with a summary of the findings below.

In terms of the state of the current, confidential project, I have designed and simulated a new version of Class-E inverter, which outputs 500W at >85% efficiency at 30MHz. I completed the PCB layout with the help of my research supervisor, and we are currently validating the board's performance.
