Compact Magnetic-Cored Inductors

MIT Power Electronics Research Group · ANSYS Maxwell / Icepak

I developed and compared compact magnetic-cored inductor designs in electromagnetic and thermal simulations, balancing physical size against quality factor.

Size versus quality factorPublication

Size versus quality factor

Relative to an earlier design, the simulated variants reduced volume by 50% with less than 20% degradation in quality factor (Q). The comparison is simulation-based; it is not presented as measured prototype performance.

Keeping both numbers together matters: a smaller inductor is only useful if its electrical performance remains acceptable. My contribution focused on exploring that tradeoff with Maxwell and Icepak models.

Publication

I am second author on the resulting paper published in the IEEE Open Journal of Power Electronics. The paper provides the detailed research context and results.
