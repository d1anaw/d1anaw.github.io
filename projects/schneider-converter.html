Three-Phase LLC Converter Design & Analysis

Schneider Electric · June–August 2026 · Internship

I worked on the design and simulation of a 100 kW, 800 V–to–52 V three-phase LLC converter, including device selection, resonant-tank requirements, loss modeling, and Python analysis automation.

Design responsibilitiesThermal and loss modelingPython / PLECS automationScope of the work

Design responsibilities

I selected SiC/GaN devices and specified the resonant capacitance, transformer turns ratio, and inductance requirements. The magnetics manufacturer handled the remaining transformer design details. I also designed the secondary-side FET-board schematic in Cadence.

Thermal and loss modeling

I built thermal and device-loss models in PLECS. The analysis predicted 97% efficiency at nominal load. This is a modeled result, not a measured converter efficiency.

Python / PLECS automation

I built an automation flow to extract primary- and secondary-side waveform extrema and RMS values, process loss results, and generate plots. I also extracted large-signal gain curves and small-signal frequency responses at selected operating points.

The framework supported analysis of device stresses and converter behavior. These were simulation-analysis scripts rather than instrument-control software.

Scope of the work

The analysis used selected operating points rather than exhaustive operating-range validation. Compensator design was not completed during the internship. This overview describes my engineering contributions without reproducing internal schematics or proprietary implementation details.
